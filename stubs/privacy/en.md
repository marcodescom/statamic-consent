## Cookies and similar technologies

We store your choice in the cookie notice in your browser's local storage (localStorage) under the name "[storage_key, default: consent]": which categories you were asked about, your choice, the version of the notice and the time of your choice. This information does not leave your device. After twelve months, or when the notice changes, we ask you again.

Storing your choice is permitted without consent under Section 25 (2) No. 2 TDDDG because it is strictly necessary for the service you requested. The legal basis for the related processing is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is to respect your choice.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time with effect for the future. To do so, click "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page.

## Google Analytics

If you have consented, we use Google Analytics 4 on this website, a web analytics service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google"). Without your consent, Google Analytics is not loaded and your browser does not connect to Google.

**Purpose and data:** We want to understand how our website is used in order to improve content and offers. For this, Google Analytics records, for example, pages viewed, time and duration of the visit, the referring page, approximate location (country, region, city), language setting, device type, operating system, browser and screen size.

**Enhanced measurement:** [Only if enabled in the GA4 property.] Google Analytics also automatically records how far you scroll down a page, clicks on links to other websites, file downloads, searches in a search function of this website (if any) and whether you start or submit a form. What you enter into a form is not transferred to Google.

**Google signals:** [Variant A, google_signals = true (default):] We have activated Google signals. If you are signed in to your Google account and have allowed personalised advertising there, Google links the data of your visit to your Google account. This gives us aggregated information on the age, gender and interests of our visitors and shows how the website is used across devices. We only see aggregated reports, no information on individual persons. Google only shows such values above a minimum number of visitors. We do not use the data for advertising: remarketing and personalised ads are switched off. Whether Google may link your data in this way is up to you in your Google account under "My Ad Center" ([myadcenter.google.com](https://myadcenter.google.com)).

[Variant B, google_signals = false:] Google signals are switched off. Google does not link the data of your visit to your Google account. We do not use advertising features, remarketing or personalised ads.

**IP address:** Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. For visits from the EU, Google uses your IP address on servers in the EU only briefly to derive the approximate location and discards it before the data is forwarded to Google Analytics.

**Cookies and retention:** Google Analytics sets the cookies "_ga" and "_ga_<id>". They remain stored for up to two years after your last visit. Data on individual visitors and events is automatically deleted in Google Analytics after [14] months. Aggregated reports without reference to individual visitors may be kept longer.

**Legal basis:** Storing and reading information on your device is based on your consent under Section 25 (1) TDDDG, the further processing of your data on your consent under Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR.

**Data processing agreement and transfer to the USA:** We have concluded a data processing agreement with Google under Art. 28 GDPR for Google Analytics (Google Ads Data Processing Terms). [Variant A only:] Where Google links the data for Google signals to your Google account, this is governed by your settings in your Google account and by Google's privacy policy. A transfer of data to Google LLC in the USA cannot be ruled out. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework, so there is an adequacy decision of the EU Commission for these transfers (Art. 45 GDPR). In addition, Google has agreed to the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 (2) (c) GDPR).

**Withdrawal:** You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future. To do so, click "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page and choose "Reject all" or untick "Statistics" in the settings. Google Analytics is then no longer loaded, and the Google Analytics cookies of this website are deleted. The lawfulness of processing until withdrawal remains unaffected.

For more information, see Google's privacy policy: [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## External media

We embed videos and maps of other providers with a two-click solution. Before you consent, you only see a notice in their place; your browser does not connect to the provider, and preview images do not come from the provider either. "Load once" loads only this one item without storing your choice. With "Always allow" you consent to content of the category "External media" being loaded on this website without asking again; we store this choice as described above.

When content is loaded, your browser technically transfers, among other things, your IP address, the page visited, date and time, and information about your browser and device to the provider. The provider may use its own cookies or similar technologies. The legal basis is your consent under Section 25 (1) TDDDG and Art. 6 (1) (a) GDPR. You can withdraw it at any time with effect for the future via "Cookie settings" at the bottom of any page; loaded content is then replaced by the notice again.
